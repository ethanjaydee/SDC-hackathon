---
name: History signature scope
description: User requirement for technician history and computer-generated sample invoices.
---
Do not show customer sign-off or signature panels in any technician history or sample completion-proof view.

**Why:** The user repeated the request to remove sign-off panels for each technician after an earlier removal missed a remaining sample-proof component.

**How to apply:** Keep history photos and job records, but no signature UI. Sample invoices should state: “This document is computer-generated; no signature is required.”
